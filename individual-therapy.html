Individual Therapy in Nairobi & Online

Confidential, one-on-one counselling tailored to help you process trauma, heal past emotional wounds, manage anxiety or depression, and move forward with clarity.

What is Individual Therapy at Shalam Wellness? Individual therapy is a private 40-to-60-minute one-on-one session with Clare Wamaitha (CPB Kenya Registered & Licensed Counselling Psychologist), available online for KES 2,500 or in person in Nairobi CBD and Thika Road (Roasters) for KES 3,500.

Who Individual Therapy Is For

You do not have to be in crisis to benefit from therapy. Individual sessions support adults, young adults, and adolescents navigating:

  • Trauma & PTSD: Effects of sexual, physical, or emotional abuse, neglect, or distressing past experiences that continue to affect daily life.
  • Unhealed Childhood Wounds & Past Hurts: Recurring triggers, emotional reactions, or patterns rooted in early experiences that you feel ready to understand and heal.
  • Anxiety, Overthinking & Burnout: Racing thoughts, chronic worry, exhaustion, and overwhelm from personal or workplace responsibilities.
  • Depression & Low Mood: Persistent sadness, emotional emptiness, or loss of motivation.
  • Grief, Loss & Life Transitions: Navigating bereavement, separation, career shifts, identity changes, or major decisions.

What Happens in Your First Session?

  1. Safe, No-Pressure Conversation: You don't have to have it all figured out. Your first session is simply a safe space to talk through what brought you in.
  2. Exploring Goals Together: Clare listens without judgment and collaborates with you on what healing or progress looks like for your unique situation.
  3. Tailored Evidence-Based Plan: Ongoing sessions integrate Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT), Person-Centred therapy, and Psychodynamic insights at your pace.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does an individual counselling session cost in Nairobi?

At Shalam Wellness, online individual therapy sessions via secure video call are KES 2,500, and in-person sessions in Nairobi CBD or Thika Road (Roasters) are KES 3,500.

Is online therapy as effective as in-person therapy?

Yes. Online sessions offer the same professional confidentiality and evidence-based therapeutic support while providing extra flexibility and privacy for clients across Kenya and abroad.

How do I book my first session?

Use our online booking page to choose your preferred format, date, and time, or reach out directly on WhatsApp at +254 797 431 765.

Crisis Support Note: Therapy sessions are booked by appointment and are not an emergency service. If you are experiencing a mental health emergency in Kenya, please call 999 / 112, reach Kenya Red Cross on 1199, or go to the nearest hospital.
