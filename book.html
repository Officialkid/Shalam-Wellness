Privacy Policy

Effective: April 2026 · Compliant with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019

Shalam Wellness ("we", "Clare Wamaitha") is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 and the ethical guidelines of the Counsellors and Psychologists Board (CPB) of Kenya.

1. Information We Collect

When you prepare a booking request, we collect your name, phone/WhatsApp number, optional email, service type, session format, preferred date/time, and any optional note. We never collect payment card details on this website.

2. Saved Booking Drafts (Local Storage)

To prevent you from losing progress, your unfinished booking form is saved strictly on your own device's browser storage (`localStorage`). It is never sent to a server until you choose to send it, and is automatically cleared once submitted or reset.

3. WhatsApp & Third-Party Communication

Submitting your booking opens WhatsApp with your pre-filled details so you can message Clare directly. Messages sent on WhatsApp are protected by WhatsApp's end-to-end encryption and governed by WhatsApp's privacy policy.

4. Confidentiality & Minors

All client information is strictly confidential under CPB Kenya ethical standards and will never be shared with third parties except where required by law or to prevent immediate risk of harm. For clients under 18 (e.g., high school referrals), parent or legal guardian consent is required.

5. Your Rights Under the Kenya Data Protection Act

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. Contact us at shalamwellness@gmail.com or +254 797 431 765.
