Couples Therapy in Nairobi & Online

A balanced, guided space for partners to move beyond recurring conflict, repair broken trust, and deepen emotional connection.

Online & In-Person Extended Sessions (Up to 2 Hours) Nairobi CBD & Thika Road
Book Couples Therapy → Meet Clare Wamaitha

How does Couples Therapy work at Shalam Wellness? Couples therapy provides a neutral, confidential space where both partners are heard equally. Sessions may run up to 2 hours to allow meaningful dialogue and practical progress, available both online and in person in Nairobi.

Common Relationship Concerns We Work Through

  • Communication Breakdown: Feeling unheard, stuck in repetitive arguments, or withdrawing during difficult conversations.
  • Trust & Emotional Repair: Navigating hurt, broken trust, or unresolved wounds from the past.
  • Emotional Disconnection: Growing apart due to stress, parenting, work demands, or unspoken expectations.
  • Intentional Growth & Pre-Marital Preparation: Building healthy conflict resolution and shared values before major life milestones.

What to Expect in Sessions

Clare works collaboratively with both partners without taking sides. Sessions focus on understanding the underlying emotional needs beneath surface conflicts, healing past hurts, and equipping you with practical communication tools you can apply at home.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long is a couples therapy session?

Couples therapy sessions may run longer than individual sessions — up to 2 hours — so both partners have adequate time to be heard and make meaningful progress.

Can we attend couples therapy online?

Yes. Couples sessions are available both online via secure video call (including for long-distance partners) and in person at our Nairobi CBD and Thika Road (Roasters) locations.
