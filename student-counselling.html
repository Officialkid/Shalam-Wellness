Does Shalam Wellness provide official therapy letters and school referral reports? Yes. Clare Wamaitha (CPB Kenya Registered & Licensed) provides structured 10-session personal therapy letters for university counselling psychology students and formal counselling reports for high school students referred following disciplinary actions.

1. Personal Therapy Letters (Counselling Psychology Students)

Many Kenyan universities require counselling psychology students to complete personal therapy as part of their professional formation and coursework. Shalam Wellness offers a genuine, structured 10-session in-person therapeutic process focused on self-awareness, emotional growth, and personal reflection.

  • Format: In-person sessions in Nairobi CBD or Thika Road (Roasters).
  • Documentation: Upon completion of the 10 sessions, an official stamped letter is provided confirming attendance for your university department.
  • Confidentiality: Your letter confirms attendance only — the personal content discussed in your sessions remains strictly confidential.

2. High School Referral Reports

When a teenager or high school student is referred for counselling following disciplinary action such as suspension, Shalam Wellness provides compassionate psychological assessment and counselling rather than punitive lecturing.

  • Focus Areas: Emotional regulation, understanding triggers and behaviour, accountability, and practical coping skills for home and school.
  • Parental Involvement: For students under 18, parent or legal guardian consent is required, with collaborative guidance for the family.
  • Formal Report: A professional referral report is prepared for submission to the school upon completion of the sessions.
