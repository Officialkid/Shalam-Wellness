Who can book Shalam Wellness for speaking? Corporate organisations, high schools, universities, youth groups, and community institutions in Nairobi and online across Kenya. Fees are quoted per event based on audience size, delivery format, and institution type.

Who We Partner With

  • Corporate Teams & Workplaces: Burnout prevention, workplace stress management, emotional intelligence, and building psychologically safe teams.
  • High Schools & Universities: Adolescent emotional regulation, exam stress, identity, resilience, and healthy peer relationships.
  • Community & Youth Organisations: Trauma awareness, mindfulness, self-compassion, and reducing mental health stigma.

How Pricing & Booking Work

Every speaking engagement and workshop is customised to your audience's needs. Fees are determined on a case-by-case basis depending on audience size, delivery format (online or in-person), and the type of institution.
