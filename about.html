About Shalam Wellness & Clare Wamaitha

Shalam Wellness is a Nairobi-based counselling psychology practice founded by Clare Wamaitha, a Registered & Licensed Counselling Psychologist (CPB Kenya) offering confidential online and in-person therapy.

Who is behind Shalam Wellness? Shalam Wellness was founded by Clare Wamaitha, a Counselling Psychologist registered and licensed with the Counsellors and Psychologists Board (CPB) of Kenya. Sessions are available in person in Nairobi (Nairobi CBD and Thika Road — Roasters) and online via secure video call.

Education & Professional Qualifications

Clare is a Counselling Psychologist registered & licensed with the Counsellors and Psychologists Board of Kenya. She is currently pursuing a Master's degree in Counselling Psychology at USIU-Africa and holds a Bachelor's degree and Diploma in Counselling Psychology.

Her professional training is further complemented by certifications in:

  • Trauma-Informed Care — supporting clients safely through past trauma, abuse, neglect, and unhealed childhood wounds.
  • Mindfulness and Self-Compassion — practical emotional grounding and nervous-system regulation.
  • Psychological First Aid (PFA) — immediate, compassionate stabilization during distressing life events.
  • Training of Trainers (ToT) — equipping schools, corporate teams, and community groups with practical mental wellness education.

Therapeutic Approaches

With over three years of clinical experience working with children, adolescents, youth, couples, and adults, Clare tailors each therapeutic journey to the individual rather than using a one-size-fits-all template. Her evidence-based framework draws from:

  • Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT): Identifying unhelpful thought and behaviour patterns and developing practical coping tools.
  • Person-Centred Approach: Creating a warm, empathetic, non-judgmental space where you set the pace of your healing.
  • Psychodynamic & Psychoanalytic Approaches: Exploring deeper emotional roots, childhood experiences, and recurring relational patterns.

Confidentiality & Ethical Practice

Everything shared in sessions is held in strict professional confidence in accordance with the ethical guidelines of the Counsellors and Psychologists Board (CPB) of Kenya and Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019. Confidentiality is only limited in rare legal or safety exceptions, such as immediate risk of harm to yourself or others.

Important Clinical Note: Shalam Wellness provides scheduled counselling and psychological support, not 24/7 emergency medical or crisis intervention. If you or someone you know is in immediate danger, please contact Kenya emergency services (999 / 112), the Kenya Red Cross toll-free helpline (1199), or visit the nearest hospital emergency department.
